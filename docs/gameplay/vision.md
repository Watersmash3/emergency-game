# Vision

The player is an incident commander. They can direct a whole team or take a single responder off that order, and the rest of the team keeps working. Outcomes come from where apparatus is placed, who is assigned, and what equipment they have, not from a scripted checklist.

The first services are structural firefighting, then police, EMS, and technical rescue. The simulation should grow by adding systems: combustion, smoke, water, injuries, civilian behavior, and vehicle movement. Those systems read and write simulation state. They do not talk to Phaser.

The game is single-player and runs on the local machine. Optional donations or a paid release may come later. The game does not require accounts, payments, or a game server.

The training yard is the current playable slice: Engine 1 can respond, the crew can suppress the yard fire, and that incident can be saved and loaded. The next milestone is queued orders for a single responder.
